# Interview preparation reading catalog

These resources are linked from PrepDesk → Prepare → select technology and experience → Resources. The app keeps its own original exercises and answers available offline. External resources may require internet, membership, or paid access; a link does not imply endorsement or permission to reproduce their content.

The 16 additions were checked on 9 October 2026. Experience filters indicate useful preparation depth, not universal hiring requirements. Articles can contain older APIs: use the official technical references shown in the app to verify version-sensitive answers. Scrum assessments are explicitly labeled as knowledge practice rather than interview questions.

## Editorial policy

Use collections to identify topics and interview formats. Write and review original explanations, rubrics, worked examples, and follow-up scenarios for the app. Keep question IDs stable and increase content versions when published material changes. Link author pages and retain source attribution. A question collection is not evidence that a particular employer asks a given question, and experience tiers are study guidance.

The current 308 questions are a starter bank. A comprehensive syllabus for every technology still requires ongoing technical review and expansion. The reading catalog supplements the in-app question bank; it does not import every linked article's questions or answers.
