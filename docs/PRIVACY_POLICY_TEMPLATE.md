## What stays on your device

MockMentor stores your cards, bookmarks, plans, study progress, mock interview answers, and focus sessions on your device. You can use the question library, review, mock interviews, and practice exercises without an account and without an internet connection.

## AI answers and feedback

To offer AI tools without asking for an email address, the app creates an anonymous guest session with our backend provider, Supabase, the first time it goes online. This session has a random identifier and no name, email address, or contact details. It lets us apply daily usage limits.

When you use **Ask**, the app sends your question, up to six previous turns of the current chat, and the identifiers of related library questions to our server. When you request **AI feedback**, it sends the answer and follow-up notes you choose to submit. Our server adds reference material from the MockMentor question library and sends the request to Google's Gemini API to produce a reply. Our server does not store your questions, answers, or the AI's replies, and it asks Gemini not to store the conversation. Google processes the request under its Gemini API terms: **[link to Google's Gemini API terms and its stated retention for abuse monitoring]**.

Do not include personal or confidential information in questions or answers. AI replies can be inaccurate and are not hiring decisions or predictions.

## What our backend keeps

For each guest session our backend keeps the random session identifier and a count of requests per feature per day, used for usage limits. Supabase and our hosting infrastructure also keep technical request logs, such as time, IP address, and status. Retention: **[Supabase log retention for your plan; usage-count retention]**. Supabase processes this data in Australia (ap-southeast-2): **[link to Supabase privacy policy / DPA]**.

We do not install analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

## Your choices and deletion

You can export your preparation as a private backup file and clear it from your device under More → Account and data. **Delete my online guest data** permanently removes the guest session and its usage records from our backend and clears preparation data on the device.
